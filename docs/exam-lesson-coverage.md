# Haydn’s Final Exam: lesson coverage

Every built-in question is linked to a short teaching card with a worked example and a practice check. The cards teach reusable methods; students do not need to memorize the 800 answers.

The normal classroom path is **basic activity → exam lesson cards → end-of-lesson quiz**. Completed learners can reopen **Study for the exam** without replaying the basics or earning a second reward.

The question pool remains **100 questions per category**, with **40 questions / five per category** as the default exam. This document records the built-in curriculum. A teacher who changes a question’s tested material must review its teaching links; an old question ID alone cannot prove that new wording is taught.

## Coverage result

- Built-in questions: 800.
- Questions with linked teaching and practice: 800.
- Missing links: 0.
- Unknown references: 0.

Question numbers below refer to the stable category-prefixed exam IDs. For example, `001–009` under Bass clef means `bass-clef-001` through `bass-clef-009`.

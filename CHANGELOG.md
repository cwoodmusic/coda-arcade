# Changelog

## 2026-10-28 — Reliable guest button

- **Play as guest** stays in the same position while the title screen loads and remains available with a remembered login.
- Guest play skips saved-login restoration, including same-tab profiles and teacher sessions. Saved profile progress stays separate.

## 2026-10-28 — Piano keyboards in pitch lessons

- Pitch lessons now show a labeled piano keyboard with black keys in groups of two and three.
- Piano-key practice questions keep the keyboard visible. Half-step, sharp, and flat examples show the keys being compared.

## 2026-10-28 — Guest entry fix

- **Play as guest** opens the arcade even when another tab or a leftover profile marker is present.
- Guest play alongside a saved profile uses separate saves that survive refreshes in that tab. The saved profile remains untouched.

## 2026-10-27 — Play notes, classroom practice, and dance controls

- Press **P** to open a notepad for bugs and future changes. It autosaves in this browser and exports a text copy. The **Changelog** tab shows bundled release notes.
- DJ Line Dance uses a directional pad: Up above Left and Right, with Down below.
- Hard mode runs at **88 BPM**.
- Every dance shuffles its rhythm patterns across all eight bars, including the opening. Sixteenth-note runs keep one direction.
- Built-in classroom teaching steps now include **three targeted check questions**.
- End-of-lesson quizzes draw fresh questions from targeted pools and shuffle their question and choice order. An unfinished quiz resumes its exact attempt in this browser.
- Staff lessons use **Line 1–5** and **Space 1–4**, numbered from bottom to top.
- Notes in developer test mode last only for that preview; download a copy before leaving it.

## 2026-10-27 — DJ achievements and disco reflections

- Added six DJ achievements, saved XP, **First Steps** and **Midnight Headliner** DJ tracks, and earned access to shutter shades, roller skates, and a boombox.
- Existing purchases remain usable. Earned gear does not equip automatically.
- Disco reflections use elapsed time and small moving trails. Reduced motion and FX settings still apply.

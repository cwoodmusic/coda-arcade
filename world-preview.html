Sprite sheet animation

For an uploaded horizontal/grid spritesheet, set the number of columns, rows and used frames. Frames play left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

Drag image to move; drag gold corner to resize, round handle to rotate. Shift-drag to bypass snapping. Delete removes selected asset.

Your assets are saved in this browser. Export scene JSON to publish or move them to another computer.

Help · shortcuts and storage

Shift+A opens/closes. Delete removes. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z redo. Arrows nudge selected asset (Shift = 8 px). Escape closes. GIF/APNG playback depends on browser support; sprite sheets offer deterministic frame animation. Crop selects a rectangular section of the *original art* (not the whole live canvas). Imported images are embedded in exported JSON. Browser storage is local to this page/origin; GitHub won't receive edits until you publish the export.

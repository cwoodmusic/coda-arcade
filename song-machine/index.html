How to compose

Hold an instrument’s Clear button for 3 seconds to erase its notes. Undo restores your last edit (Ctrl+Z). Select a note value and click a pitch row to place it. Drag horizontally to set its duration (snaps to 16th notes). Click an existing note to remove it. All notes show notation and highlighted duration. Scroll sideways for all bars. Choose a root and scale to show only its pitch rows. Notes outside the selected scale remain in your composition and sheet music, but are hidden in the grid. Three percussion rows appear below the pitched tracks.

Your offline studio

Choose artwork on the left. Paint with a resizable pencil/eraser, draw rectangle or ellipse shapes, fill, make manual polygon or freehand lasso selections, copy, paste, stamp, feather edges, or blend colors using the same tools as the arcade. Brush size is 1–128 pixels, with round or square tips. Shapes can be outlines or solid fills. The blend brush has radius and strength controls. Use [ and ] over the canvas to change pencil size. Shortcuts: B pencil, E eraser, L line, R rectangle, O ellipse, F fill, M select, U blend, I eyedropper, S stamp. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes. Right-drag or hold Space to pan. Use Fit image for large rooms.

Choose a character view to change every wardrobe option and paint their pixels. Each direction has its own edits; the four previews show animation. Pet companions are also separate editable artwork.

Export PNG saves the selected picture. Export edits JSON saves every changed asset, character look, and direction together. Open edits continues that work in this editor. Export regularly; browser drafts may be limited by browser storage.

The artwork file is for applying through the assistant, not for importing as a world layout. Library/source-sheet edits and new canvases retain their own identities. It contains no accounts or player saves.
